## About

I am completing my Master of Engineering in Computer Science at **Cornell Tech**.
My work sits around applied AI, ML systems, robotics-adjacent tooling, and
infrastructure that makes intelligent software more useful in practice.

I enjoy starting from speculative product ideas, then forcing them into working
systems: real sensors, real APIs, real latency, real failure modes.
